I. Tenses in Context

Read the dialogue to answer questions 1-4:

Chloe: How is the community garden project going?
Daniel: Great! 1. We __________ all the vegetable seeds yesterday morning.
Chloe: That was fast! 2. __________ the new irrigation system yet?
Daniel: No, but the technician is on his way. He 3. __________ on similar systems for over ten years before he started his own company.
Chloe: Perfect. By the time summer arrives, we 4. __________ fresh tomatoes!
1. Choose the correct verb for blank (1):
  • A. have planted
  • B. planted
  • C. are planting
  • D. had planted
  • E. plant
Answer: B. planted (Past Simple - indicates a completed action with a specific past time "yesterday morning").
2. Choose the correct verb for blank (2):
  • A. Do you install
  • B. Did you install
  • C. Have you installed
  • D. Are you installing
  • E. Will you install
Answer: C. Have you installed (Present Perfect - asking about an action relevant to the present moment, cued by "yet").
3. Choose the correct verb for blank (3):
  • A. has worked
  • B. works
  • C. worked
  • D. had worked
  • E. was working
Answer: D. had worked (Past Perfect - action completed *before* another past action "started his own company").
4. Choose the correct verb for blank (4):
  • A. will have harvested
  • B. are harvesting
  • C. harvest
  • D. will be harvesting
  • E. have harvested
Answer: D. will be harvesting (Future Continuous - ongoing action at a specific future time. *Will have harvested* is also grammatically correct, but D fits the active anticipation better).

II. Pronoun & Reference

Wind turbines are a crucial source of renewable energy. 5. _______ generate electricity without emitting greenhouse gases. Environmentalists argue that expanding 6. _______ infrastructure is the key to combating climate change.
5. Fill in blank (5):
  • A. It
  • B. We
  • C. They
  • D. This
  • E. That
Answer: C. They (Refers back to the plural noun "Wind turbines").
6. Fill in blank (6):
  • A. those
  • B. these
  • C. that
  • D. this
  • E. there
Answer: D. this (Demonstrative pronoun indicating the singular "infrastructure" related to the turbines just mentioned).

III. Modal Verbs

7. When visiting a historical temple, tourists ___ wear modest clothing out of respect for local customs.
  • A. might
  • B. shouldn't
  • C. must
  • D. wouldn't
  • E. can
Answer: C. must (Expresses a strong obligation or rule).
8. You ___ take photographs using a flash inside the museum, as it can damage the ancient paintings.
  • A. don't have to
  • B. mustn't
  • C. should
  • D. might not
  • E. ought to
Answer: B. mustn't (Expresses strict prohibition).

  • C. quitting
  • D. quitted
  • E. quits
Answer: B. to quit (The verb 'decide' is followed by a to-infinitive).

VII. Reading Comprehension (Historical Recount)

The Space Race was a 20th-century competition between two Cold War rivals, the Soviet Union and the United States, to achieve superior spaceflight capability. It originated from the nuclear arms race following World War II. The technological superiority required for such dominance was seen as necessary for national security. The Soviets achieved early successes, including launching the first artificial satellite, Sputnik 1, in 1957. This prompted the U.S. government to dramatically increase funding for science education and create NASA. The race peaked on July 20, 1969, when the U.S. successfully landed Apollo 11 on the Moon and Neil Armstrong took his historic first steps. This event effectively ended the race, demonstrating America's technological triumph and shifting global power dynamics.
18. What is the main idea of the text?
  • A. The Soviet Union won the Space Race by launching Sputnik.
  • B. Neil Armstrong was the first man to fly an airplane.
  • C. The Space Race was a technological competition driven by national security that culminated in the Moon landing.
  • D. NASA was created to build nuclear weapons.
  • E. World War II was fought primarily in outer space.
Answer: C (Accurately summarizes the core narrative of the passage).
19. According to the text, why did the U.S. government create NASA?
  • A. Because they wanted to collaborate with the Soviet Union.
  • B. Because of the early successes of the Soviet Union, like Sputnik 1.
  • C. To establish a colony on Mars.
  • D. To reduce the federal budget for science.
  • E. To negotiate the end of World War II.
Answer: B (Prompted by Soviet successes to achieve technological superiority).
20. What can be inferred about the impact of the Apollo 11 landing?
  • A. It proved that the Soviet Union had better technology.
  • B. It caused the immediate start of World War III.
  • C. It established the United States as the dominant technological power.
  • D. It led to the immediate defunding of NASA.
  • E. It proved that the moon was made of artificial materials.
Answer: C ("demonstrating America's technological triumph and shifting global power dynamics").
