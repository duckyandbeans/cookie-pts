Grade 11 (XI) Study Checklist

Module 1: Vocabulary & Reading

Sports & Well-being

  • 🔑 Key Terms: Mindful, fortitude, resilience, emotional regulation, grit, adversity, self-esteem.
  • 🎯 The Goal: Be able to explain how these mental traits help an athlete more than just physical strength.
  • ⚠️ Watch Out For: Words that look similar but mean different things (e.g., self-esteem vs. self-confidence).

Module 2: The Passive Voice

  • 🔑 Concept: Subject + Be (in correct tense) + Past Participle (V3).
  • Tenses to Review:
    • Past Simple: The match was won.
    • Present Continuous: The routines are being practiced.
    • Present Perfect: Records have been broken.
  • ⚠️ Watch Out For: Irregular verbs! (e.g., Win → Won, Catch → Caught, Break → Broken).

Module 3: Data & Graph Literacy

🔑 Terminology:

Upward trend, peak, fluctuation, stable, significant increase.

🎯 The Goal:

Look at a graph and identify which year had the most growth or which group is the smallest.

⚠️ Watch Out For:

The difference between "Quantity" (how many) and "Percentage" (the rate).

Grade 12 (XII) Study Checklist

Module 1: Advanced Vocabulary

Identity & Society

  • 🔑 Key Terms: Uprooted, formative years, put down roots, cultural identity, conurbation.
  • 🎯 The Goal: Use these in sentences about moving to a new city or keeping your traditions alive.
  • ⚠️ Watch Out For: The phrasal verb settle down—it means to make a home, not just to be quiet.

Module 2: Advanced Passive & Reporting

  • 🔑 Impersonal Passives: It is said that... / It is believed that...
  • 🔑 Reporting Structure: The artifact is claimed to be...
  • 🎯 The Goal: Change "People say he is a genius" into "He is said to be a genius."
  • ⚠️ Watch Out For: "There is..." vs "It is...". Use "There is thought to be..." when talking about the existence of something.

Module 3: Conditionals & Negation

  • 🔑 Conditionals: Review Type 1 (If I study, I will pass) and Type 2 (If I lived in London, I would...).
  • Negation Rules:
    • Neither... nor: The verb follows the second subject.
    • None of: Focus on the whole group.
    • Not only... but also: Keep both sides balanced.
  • ⚠️ Watch Out For: Using "nor" with "neither" (never "neither... or").

Module 4: Relative Clauses & Logic

  • 🔑 Commas:
    • Essential info (Defining): No commas.
    • Extra info (Non-defining): MUST have commas.
  • 🔑 Formal Pronouns: Using Whom (for objects) and Which (to refer to a whole previous sentence).
  • 🔑 Sentence Ordering: Practice starting with a broad topic and narrowing down to specific details or conclusions.

Module 5: Technology & Cryptography

🔑 Key Concepts:
  • Symmetric: One key (fast, but risky to share).
  • Asymmetric: Two keys (Public and Private).
🎯 The Goal:

Explain why "Asymmetric" is generally more secure for internet banking.

⚠️ Watch Out For:

The definition of encryption (it’s the process, not just the password).

True / False / Not Given (TFNG) Strategy

✅

TRUE

The text says exactly this. The meaning matches perfectly, even if synonyms are used.

❌

FALSE

The text says the opposite or clearly contradicts the statement.

🤷

NOT GIVEN

The text doesn't give you enough info to decide. (Even if you think it's true in real life, if it's not in the text, it's Not Given!)
