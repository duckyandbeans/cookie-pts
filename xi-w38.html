Mastering Reporting Verbs

A bilingual guide (English & Indonesian) to academic reading comprehension

1. Neutral & Factual Verbs

Used to present information without strong judgment or bias.

State
Menyatakan
To express something clearly and definitively in speech or writing.
Describe
Mendeskripsikan
To give a detailed account of characteristics or events.
Observe
Mengamati
To notice or perceive something and register it as being significant.
Examine
Memeriksa / Meneliti
To inspect something in detail to determine its nature or condition.
Illustrate
Mengilustrasikan / Memberi contoh
To explain or make something clear by using examples, charts, or pictures.

Checkpoint Quiz 1: Neutral Verbs

Question 1.1: The author includes a detailed chart showing population growth over the last 50 years to make the complex demographic shift easier to understand. Which verb best describes the author's action?
Question 1.2: A professional laboratory technician spends three weeks inspecting cellular structures under a microscope to catalog hidden structural anomalies. The technician is trying to ________ the cells.
Question 1.3: An eye-witness gives a pure chronological, play-by-play account detailing the sequence of vehicle movements before the crash, without offering personal judgments on liability. The witness is ________ the event.
Question 1.4: An astronomer notes a single, sudden, unexpected spike on a radiation graph during a routine deep-space scan and quickly logs the coordinates. The astronomer ________ the spike.
Question 1.5: The industrial equipment guidebook strictly prints: "All users must disengage the primary power line prior to clearing an internal jam." The guidebook explicitly ________ this operational protocol.
Question 1.6: To introduce the wide-reaching dangers of sudden regional deindustrialization, an economist writes a short case study on the economic trajectory of a single mining village in Wales. The economist uses this case study to ________ the issue.

2. Argumentative & Assertive Verbs

Used when the author is taking a firm stand or making a definitive point.

Argue
Berargumen
To give reasons or cite evidence in support of an idea, action, or theory.
Assert
Menegaskan
To state a fact or belief confidently and forcefully, often without immediate proof.
Emphasize
Menekankan
To give special importance or prominence to something in speaking or writing.
Advocate
Menganjurkan / Mendukung
To publicly recommend or support a particular cause or policy action.
Contend
Mempertahankan pendapat
To assert something firmly as a specific position within an ongoing debate.

Checkpoint Quiz 2: Argumentative Verbs

Question 2.1: An environmental activist publishes an opinion piece urgently pushing city planners to zone out cars from municipal centers completely. Which verb captures this action?
Question 2.2: Without presenting verified fiscal charts, local tax receipts, or statistical support, a candidate confidently declares, "Our regional economic infrastructure is flawless." The candidate is ________ this claim.
Question 2.3: In a comprehensive 40-page journal paper, a sociologist weaves historical census records, cross-sectional asset data, and field interviews to prove that systemic wealth disparity alters local educational outcomes. The author ________ this thesis.
Question 2.4: During her public address, the coordinator repeats the phrase "immediate evacuation" three separate times and points emphatically to the map exit markers. The coordinator is trying to ________ the exit routes.
Question 2.5: Even when faced with intense counter-arguments from the peer review panel, the lead demographer firmly keeps their ground, holding that their original data filtering methods were fully valid. The demographer ________ that their method was correct.
Question 2.6: A coalition of non-profit legal professionals builds an official website and runs ads targeting lawmakers to demand a reform of youth prison sentences. The coalition is ________ for prison reform.

3. Tentative & Speculative Verbs

Used when dealing with hypotheses, or leaving room for doubt.

Suggest
Mengisyaratkan / Menyarankan
To put forward an idea gently for consideration; to hint at a correlation.
Hypothesize
Menghipotesiskan
To put forward a structured, unproven proposition as a starting point for scientific testing.
Speculate
Berspekulasi / Menduga
To form a loose theory or opinion based on highly incomplete or missing evidence.
Imply
Menyiratkan
To choose framing that guides the reader to a conclusion without saying it out loud.
Postulate
Mendalilkan
To assume or establish the truth of a core rule or axiom as a starting baseline for logical reasoning.

Checkpoint Quiz 3: Tentative Verbs

Question 3.1: An investigator notes an unusual, tiny mark at the crime scene but doesn't have lab confirmation. She notes, "It remains within the realm of possibility that an unlisted compound was present." The investigator is ________.
Question 3.2: Prior to turning on the particle injector, the engineering team writes an operational thesis stating that increasing variable X will alter compound stability. They then prepare a set of 10 experimental runs to test this. The team is ________.
Question 3.3: A profile writer notes, "The CFO purchased three massive beachfront estates this winter, all while the company's employee retirement fund saw unexplained drops," stopping short of direct accusations. The writer is ________ misconduct.
Question 3.4: In a calm tone, a consultant notes, "The data curves look slightly positive; perhaps we could see a marginal rise in Q4 if shipping costs hold." The consultant is ________ a potential revenue trend.
Question 3.5: To construct a new model of market mechanics, macroeconomic theorists declare that all consumers act with perfect rationality. They don't prove this statement; they treat it as an unshakeable axiom to allow their sub-formulas to run. The theorists ________ this consumer behavior.
Question 3.6: Without checking verified tech specs, a technology podcaster guesses that a competitor's upcoming phone will fail because of an unverified internal cooling rumor. The podcaster is ________.

4. Opposing & Critical Verbs

Used to break down arguments, disagree, or track debates.

Refute
Membantah / Mematahkan
To absolutely prove an assertion or theory to be false using undeniable empirical evidence.
Dispute
Memperdebatkan / Menyanggah
To question the validity, accuracy, or foundational honesty of an alleged fact or source.
Concede
Mengakui kebenaran / Mengalah
To admit that a specific point made by an opponent is true or valid, usually before launching a counter-argument.
Dismiss
Mengabaikan / Menepis
To reject an idea entirely without giving it prolonged analytical consideration because it lacks basic credibility.
Reject
Menolak
To refuse to accept, adopt, or believe an input, idea, or proposal.

Checkpoint Quiz 4: Critical Verbs

Question 4.1: A modern historian evaluates an old 19th-century text. She agrees that the textbook's record of weapon manufacturing numbers is completely accurate, but then proceeds to tear apart the book's broad causal arguments. Regarding the manufacturing numbers, the historian ________.
Question 4.2: For generations, textbooks argued that a specific localized flood destroyed the ancient citadel. However, Dr. Lin presents deep sediment core samples proving that rainfall was entirely absent for a century. Dr. Lin effectively ________ the flood theory.
Question 4.3: The defense representative targets the credibility of the primary witness, highlighting that the street lamps were completely broken and visibility was near zero. The representative is ________ the witness's visual record.
Question 4.4: The executive board throws out a fringe proposal after reading only the title line, labeling it "an absurd fantasy with zero financial grounding." The board ________ the concept.
Question 4.5: Following a formal breakdown of the safety clauses, the laborer association casts an absolute 'nay' ballot and pushes the document away. The association ________ the framework agreement.
Question 4.6: While the film reviewer explicitly accepts that the cinema project had "truly stunning visual composition," they immediately pivot to tearing apart the weak dialogue. Regarding the visual composition, the reviewer ________.

The Drama Circle: Secret Role Game

The Drama Circle

You overheard 2 ex best friends talking. Now you need to convert their direct speech into reported speech! (Because you are gossiping to another friend, hahaha)

Student A: Cassandra's Task

Read your direct speech items line-by-line out loud to your partner. Convert them to reported speech together, check the box, and review your reporting verb options!

Line 1: "OMG girl, I am telling you Leonardo is definitely cheating on you with Miranda."
|
Academic Options: asserts, claims, alleges, contends
Example: Cassandra claims that Leonardo is cheating on Lisa.
Line 2: "I saw him walk out of the movie theater with her yesterday."
|
Academic Options: states, notes, observes, reports
Example: She states that she witnessed him walking out of the theater.
Line 3: "This is so crazy because you guys are going to get married soon!"
|
Academic Options: exclaims, remarks, comments, points out
Example: Cassandra exclaims that the situation is wild given their wedding plan.
Line 4: "I actually scream at the both of them once I was close enough to them."
|
Academic Options: admits, describes, recounts, states
Example: She recounts shouting at both individuals in public.
Line 5: "I told them you guys are so shameless and that Leonardo doesn't deserve you."
|
Academic Options: criticizes, insults, slams, dismisses
Example: She openly criticizes Leonardo's actions and character.

Student B: Lisa's Task

Read your direct speech items line-by-line out loud to your partner. Convert them to reported speech together, check the box, and review your reporting verb options!

Line 1: "Shut up, Cassandra stop lying. Leonardo was literally by my side yesterday."
|
Academic Options: refutes, disputes, rejects, denies
Example: Lisa immediately refutes Cassandra's tracking claims.
Line 2: "Anastasia has told me all about you and that you're trying to break us up just to have him."
|
Academic Options: reveals, exposes, counters, discloses
Example: Lisa reveals that Anastasia uncovered Cassandra's secret intentions.
Line 3: "I thought you were my best friend and I thought that I could trust you, but you're just a two face backstabbing degenerate."
|
Academic Options: condemns, attacks, brands, labels
Example: She completely condemns Cassandra as a dishonest peer.
Line 4: "I literally took a photo with Leonardo yesterday so you need to stop lying."
|
Academic Options: demonstrates, illustrates, proves, corroborates
Example: Lisa points to a photograph to corroborate her partner's presence.
Line 5: "You need to find a therapist. You're so awful."
|
Academic Options: suggests, urges, recommends, advises
Example: Lisa sharply suggests that Cassandra seek counseling.
