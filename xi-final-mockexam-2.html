Directions: Read the text about "The Demographics of Global Migration" and answer questions 21 - 31.
The Demographics of Global Migration

Paragraph 1: In contemporary geopolitics, patterns of cross-border human movement have become a ubiquitous feature of global discourse, transforming the demographics of receiving states. Historically, the underlying premise of international law was that physical barriers would successfully regulate these sudden structural shifts. Notwithstanding strict policy frameworks, the volume of global immigration continues to reach unprecedented historical thresholds. Geographic displacement forces modern nation-states to manage highly diverse populations. Driven by survival, the contemporary migrant navigates complex legal hurdles and slow bureaucratic checkpoints just to build a viable life.

Paragraph 2: The domestic political response to these shifting demographics is rarely unified. Conversely, while humanitarian factions encourage aid, alternative groups express severe anxiety regarding infrastructure strain. They argue that sudden, unmanaged population growth places a pervasive burden on the public health apparatus and municipal housing programs. However, economists argue that complaints regarding job loss often lack solid empirical support. When integration is handled cleanly, foreign laborers balance local markets, meaning that native anxieties over complete economic displacement become entirely superfluous.

Paragraph 3: Paradoxically, an overly restrictive border regime can accidentally increase a fleeing family's reliance on dangerous human smuggling networks. If legal processing channels are clogged, border facilities suffer an anomalous backlog. This logistical breakdown effectively stalls human lives, trapping vulnerable people in an agonizing legal limbo for months. Resolving these overlapping crises will require sweeping structural overhauls that leave a profound mark on global policy. Reforming these systems remains an essential, albeit highly sensitive, geopolitical challenge.
